How Augury works
  1. Brief — you describe the product, budget, goal and any channels that are off-limits.
  2. Plan — each cycle the agent proposes how to split the budget across channels.
  3. Approve — nothing runs until you approve. You can edit line items first.
  4. Launch & Measure — channels run for their evaluation window.
  5. Reflect — each channel gets a verdict: SCALE, HOLD, CUT or INSUFFICIENT DATA.
Teal = agent activity & positive results
Orange = something needs your decision
